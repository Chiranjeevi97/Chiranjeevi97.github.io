Morning Assistant

A private assistant that has your day sorted before you open your inbox.

Morning Assistant is a personal tool built and used by its owner only. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Every weekday morning it prepares a short brief and sends it as a direct message in a private Slack workspace.

What the brief contains

  • Today’s calendar, with conflicts and back-to-back meetings flagged
  • The few emails that need a reply, with optional draft replies saved to Gmail
  • Tasks due today
  • The local weather forecast
  • A handful of news headlines from trusted sources

How it uses Google data

It reads the owner’s Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks with read-only access, and can create Gmail drafts. It never sends email on its own. All processing happens on the owner’s own computer.
